# Real Interview Intelligence

> Web-sourced candidate reports with source URLs for sanity-checking. Volume of NAB India reports is decent for SWE/Data Engineer, thin for "Platform Engineer" specifically — see the honest caveat at the bottom.

---

## Process shape

- **Process shape matches the recruiter's description.** Codility online assessment with three coding questions — e.g., a binary string reduction problem needing an optimized solution, an array constraint-validation problem, and a logic/edge-case question — with candidates choosing a Java-based (SWE) or Python-based (Data Engineer) track.
  Source: Glassdoor, Nov 2025 candidate report — https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/NAB-National-Australia-Bank-Software-Engineer-Interview-Questions-EI_IE4262.0,27_KO28,45.htm
- **Technical F2F depth:** same Glassdoor report describes a ~45-minute in-depth technical round covering core OOP, then DSA across arrays, strings, stacks, queues, linked lists, trees, graphs, and DP, including Dijkstra's and frequent time/space complexity questions — with a significant portion devoted to project discussion, implementation details, design choices, and notably **how well the candidate understood NAB as a company and its domain**. Prep a "why NAB / what NAB does" answer. ([[nab-nef-context|NAB, NEF and Banking Domain Context]]; DSA refreshers: [[lp-dsa|Learning Path - DSA]])
- **HR/behavioral round:** ~20 minutes, highly situation-based — dealing with a non-performing teammate, failures, critical feedback received, personal/family background. (Same Glassdoor source.) ([[behavioral-questions|Behavioral and Management Round Questions]])
- **Overall difficulty:** Glassdoor rates NAB SWE interviews 3/5 difficulty, 75% positive experience. (Same source.)

## Codility specifics

- **Codility difficulty/cutoff (anecdotal, unverified):** one Fishbowl candidate reported "2 medium and 1 hard DSA problems, ~67% needed to clear"; a DevOps-profile candidate confirmed even DevOps hires get the same DSA-style Codility test.
  Source: https://www.fishbowlapp.com/post/have-codility-test-with-nab-national-bank-of-australiafor-automation-tester-profile-can-anyone-please-help-me-as-to-what and https://www.fishbowlapp.com/post/has-anyone-given-nab-codility-test-whats-the-difficulty-level
- **Feedback speed (anecdotal):** first-round Codility feedback reportedly same-day (~3 hours); slower after round two.
  Source: Glassdoor community thread — https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Community/nab-recruitment-process

## Adjacent tracks

- **Data Engineer track (relevant since it's the Python-based track):** Interview Query's NAB guide recommends readiness on SQL, Python, Airflow, DBT, AWS services; STAR-format behavioral answers around competing priorities, delivering bad news, and ownership; describes the process as friendly and conversational with strong emphasis on cultural fit and "customers first" values.
  Source: https://www.interviewquery.com/interview-guides/nab-data-engineer

## Honest caveat

No candidate report specifically labeled "Platform Engineer, NEF, Bangalore" was found. The pattern above (Codility DSA → deep technical + project discussion → situational HR/management) is the best-supported proxy; the platform-specific technical content in the question banks is inference from the JD, not candidate reports.
