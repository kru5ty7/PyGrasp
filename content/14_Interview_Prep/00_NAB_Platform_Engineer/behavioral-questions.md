# Behavioral and Management Round Questions

> Sourced pattern: NAB's HR round is situation-heavy per [[real-interview-intelligence|Real Interview Intelligence]]; specific questions below are inferred from that pattern + standard senior banking-tech interviews. Prep all in STAR format — Interview Query specifically recommends STAR for NAB, flagging competing priorities, delivering bad news, and ownership as themes.

---

## Ownership

- A component owned end-to-end — concept to production to on-call.
- A time you saw a problem outside your remit and fixed it anyway.
- A time you shipped something later realized to be wrong — what did you do?

## Incidents & production

- Worst production incident: detection → triage → mitigation → RCA → prevention. (The resume claims zero downtime incidents — still prepare a near-miss or hard incident; "never had one" reads as evasive at a bank.)
- Roll back vs fix forward under pressure — how do you decide? (Technical backing: [[cicd-design-questions|CI/CD Design Question Bank]] deployment strategies.)
- A time your change broke something for another team.
- Running/contributing to a blameless post-mortem.

## Stakeholders & developer experience

- A "customer" team pushed back on your platform/tool — handling adoption resistance.
- Gathering requirements from teams with conflicting needs. (The 30+ enterprise clients story fits.)
- Delivering bad news: a deadline slipping, or a requested feature being a bad idea.
- Disagreement with a senior engineer or manager on a technical decision. (The reported HR round asked about a non-performing teammate and receiving critical feedback — prep both specifically.)

## Judgment & risk (banking flavor)

- A time you slowed a delivery for security/quality — how did you justify it? ([[pipeline-security-compliance|Pipeline Security and Compliance]] gives the vocabulary.)
- Balancing shipping fast against risk in a regulated environment.
- A time you had to say no.

## Standard management-round items

- Why leave Deltek after 4 years? (Positive, growth-framed.)
- Why platform engineering as a direction, given the integration background?
- 3-year plan; failures and lessons; salary expectation (18 LPA fixed was told to the recruiter — stay consistent).
- Personal/family background questions appeared in the reported HR round — common in India-based bank HR rounds; keep answers brief and comfortable.
